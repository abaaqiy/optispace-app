## Review limits

Only explicit code/title pairs are included. Shared headings are retained as printed; combined codes are split only where the source explicitly names both. Credit columns, prerequisites, staff assignments and prose were removed. Truncated or malformed entries were omitted where the title could not be established. Some source spelling is retained; an admin should correct it against the applicable curriculum.

Known disagreements include CSC102 (Computing / Cybersecurity / Software Engineering), CLI601 across health programmes, ICH401/ICH405 between Chemistry tables and descriptions, and several codes within the Software Engineering file. Alternatives and their physical PDF pages stay visible; no candidate is selected automatically.

Faculty-wide and borrowed health/computing entries do not establish the owning department. When ownership is uncertain, the suggestion preserves the department already entered in the review form.

Undergraduate and postgraduate titles are included where explicitly listed. B.Pharm records are historical and **not verified for PharmD**. Effective sessions must be checked by the administrator. No percentage accuracy has been measured.

The Law file is an OAU Law Students’ Society Academic Committee compilation (2016/2017), not a verified current faculty-issued handbook. For example, JPL501/JPL502 appear as Jurisprudence in one section and Conveyancing in another, while the table lists JPL503/JPL504 for Conveyance. Both explicit alternatives retain their page references and a warning.
